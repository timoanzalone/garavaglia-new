      <p><b>Edutainment HUB was created to produce innovative entertainment
      formats of the highest level</b>, combining partners’ experience and
      creativity to deliver engaging new concepts for the increasingly
      sophisticated audiences of large shopping centres, outlets and parks.</p>
      <p>The HUB will capture the most popular themes in art, archaeology,
      history, science, the environment and society, identifying the most
      qualified partners in each field to turn these themes into “experience”
      events/exhibitions that — through the most innovative educational
      communication techniques — can capture audiences with emotionally rich
      storytelling.</p>
      <p>In exhibition formats, visitors are led on true “journeys through time”,
      learning the latest discoveries across fields of knowledge through emotion.
      They can witness a gladiator fight in the Colosseum of 2,000 years ago,
      stand beside young Marco Polo on his adventurous journey to China, or
      discover how an archaeologist descending into the dark of a pyramid finds
      themselves before the mystery of a mummy.</p>
      <p>Edutainment Hub projects and initiatives are also available in multimedia
      versions.</p>
      <p>We have developed digital paths and immersive realities that can also be
      experienced remotely — comfortably at home or in the classroom with
      classmates and teachers.</p>
