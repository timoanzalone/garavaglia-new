<p>For down-town Arese we have created an impressive and luminous Christmas set-up with events and entertainment. </p><p>The colour red has been used as a theme for creating a suggestive Christmas atmosphere which decorates an artificial tree measuring 13 meters high together with other smaller real trees and holly positioned on the balconies throughout the centre.</p>
<p>Even the children’s area has been decorated with festive lights, colours and the great throne for Santa Claus to take pictures in the full Christmas spirit. </p>
