<p>To make the magic of Christmas come alive in one of the most modern and suggestive squares in Milan, we placed an impressive tree measuring about 13 meters high and richly decorated with white-cream baubles and thousands of lights.</p>
<p>This luminous event is sponsored by Esselunga. Throughout the Christmas period, the area will be set up, decorated and hosting events and displays. </p>
