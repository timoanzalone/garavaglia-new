<p>During the weekend dated 15th-18th July, Sforzesco Castle hosted an event related to the Game of Thrones 7 and welcomed participants from the Marathon who were dedicated to the famous series, with fascinating set ups and animations. </p><p>Dragons, monsters and weird creatures populated the gardens of the Castle creating a magical atmosphere. </p>
<p>The set design was entirely created by our sculptors who interpreted the characters, creatures and symbols from this world renowned fantasy saga in the most realistic way. </p>
