<p>For the opening of its new rotisserie, Iper La Grande asked us to create something unique, ironic and memorable.<br> We therefore worked on a customer experience project to welcome customers and entertain them.</p>
<p>From the hands of our artists and sculptors came many chicken chefs!<br> Each with a different dish to offer, each with his own particular expression that will bring a smile to your face.</p>
