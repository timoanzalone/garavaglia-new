<p>The world of arts and crafts in one great scenographic space made of games, installations and interactive stimuli to discover “what to do when you grow up” having fun and playing with other children in complete safety. The playing area was created as if it were miniature city where shops, activities and areas are dedicated to various trades and jobs, which appear from street to street. From the pastry shop, to the beauty salon, from the police station to the fire department, from the dental clinic to the mechanic, the children can become protagonists of fantastic adventures projected into their future. </p>
<p>The scenery that makes up this unique and homely area, has been carved and coloured by our craftsmen with meticulous care for each and every detail. The structure and specific details relating to each job, shows how children’s imagination can be allowed to create rich and exciting stories. The different areas host instalments and playgrounds created to stimulate the ability of the children’s’ physical strengths and balance but also digital and interactive activities to intrigue and make new discoveries. Through the latest generation of touch screens, children will be stimulated by various types and levels of educational games. </p>
